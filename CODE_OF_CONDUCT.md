# Code of conduct

Contributors are expected to be technically rigorous, respectful, and clear about uncertainty.

Please:

- challenge claims and implementations without attacking people;
- cite primary Apple or Swift sources for version-sensitive guidance;
- welcome corrections, especially around accessibility, privacy, device behavior, and release policy;
- disclose when a result is simulated, inferred, fixture-based, or not physically verified;
- keep discussion focused on making native Apple development more reliable.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, credential sharing, and deliberately misleading release or safety claims are not welcome.
